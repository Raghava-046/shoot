What is an AI reel generator?

An AI reel generator is a tool designed to help create short-form videos for platforms such as Instagram. Instead of starting with a blank editing timeline, a creator can use source material such as product details, website copy and brand assets to develop a video concept.

SHOOTZA is being developed around a website-first workflow: use a business website as the starting point for marketing content ideas, with the goal of making short-form video creation simpler for small businesses, creators and brands.

How SHOOTZA is designed to work

  1. Start with a website. Add your business or product website as the source for brand and product information.
  2. Build a video concept. SHOOTZA is planned to help turn that information into a script and branded visual direction.
  3. Prepare social content. The product direction includes short vertical video concepts, matching post ideas and captions for social media.

Why start with your website?

Your website often contains the basics needed to understand a business: what it sells, who it serves, its product descriptions and its visual identity. SHOOTZA's planned approach uses that information as a starting point, so businesses do not have to explain their brand from scratch every time they plan a piece of content.
